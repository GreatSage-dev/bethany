Faithfulness & Responsible AI

            
Honest about what AI should never do

            

              AI understands messy spoken voicemails. Plain deterministic code decides crisis handoffs, privacy boundaries, and human approvals.
            

          


          

            

              01
              

                
Zero synthetic preaching or counseling

                
Pressing 1 streams your own pastor's recorded audio. Barna and Gloo research (Oct 2026) found 94% of pastors worry about AI misinterpreting Scripture and 71% to 94% demand immediate human handoff in spiritual or self-harm crises.

              

            


            

              02
              

                
Deterministic crisis circuit breaker

                
Self-harm, abuse, medical falls, and acute distress are detected by deterministic rules before any LLM prompt runs. Urgent calls cannot be approved into the volunteer errand pool (enforced with HTTP 409).

              

            


            

              03
              

                
Server-side RBAC and Rock#7183 immunity

                
Volunteer payloads physically omit raw transcripts, phone numbers, doctors, street addresses, and medications on the server. Unknown caller lookups always return an empty list rather than dumping congregation care records.

              

            


            

              04
              

                
No ChMS migration, under $10 a month to run

                
Approved visits export directly to Genesis ChMS and Planning Center People CSV formats with spreadsheet formula sanitization, keeping your congregation's existing tools intact.
